## Executive Summary
**Overview**

Finance and Marketing requested an analysis of ecommerce user and transaction data from 2019–2026 to identify the primary areas where investment should be focused in 2027, paying close attention to current trends in 2026 as the year closes. This analytical report is summarized in the Executive findings and recommendations tab, with further discussion below and images of the dashboard deliverables at the end of the report. 

The analysis evaluated customer purchasing behavior, product performance, marketing traffic sources, geographic markets, and gross profit to distinguish between areas with an established revenue base and areas with potential for future growth.

### Overall Findings & Recommendations

For 2027, investment should be concentrated across four primary areas: customer retention, established geographic markets, high-performing product categories, and scalable marketing channels.

The strongest opportunity is to increase the value of the company's existing customer base. Repeat customers generated an over 50% increase in revenue in 2026, approximately $1.7 million and almost $900 million in gross profit, making them the largest customer group by total financial contribution. At the same time, over 25,000 one-time customers this year alone represent a substantial pool of customers who could potentially be converted into repeat buyers.

Geographically, China, the United States, and Brazil are the strongest established markets, collectively generating more than half of total revenue from 2019-2026. These markets provide an existing customer and revenue base on which additional investment can build. South Korea represents a smaller but rapidly growing market that may warrant controlled expansion testing in 2027.

Product demand is concentrated in a relatively small number of categories. Outerwear & Coats, Jeans, Sweaters, Swim & Suits, and Sports Coats collectively account for nearly 30% of revenue in 2026. Investment in inventory, merchandising, and marketing should look into holding onto well performing categories, and possibly seek what other products can be uplifted or dropped altogether.

From a marketing perspective, Email and AdWords currently generate the most revenue and gross profit. These channels should remain core components of the marketing strategy, while Organic traffic represents a longer-term opportunity for SEO investment in 2027.

Overall, the recommended strategy is to protect proven revenue sources while using targeted testing to identify the next areas of scalable growth:

### Priority 1: Conversion to Repeat Purchases
**Findings**

Customer retention represents one of the strongest opportunities for 2027.

<img width="3007" height="339" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d4b061f1-fdbe-4c21-9f2f-3bc4cf08f06f" />

Repeat customers generated approximately $1.76 million in total overall revenue in 2026 as well as $884K in gross profit, making them the largest contributor among the three customer groups.

<img width="3156" height="2176" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fdfaf09d-17a6-4515-adc9-473edb401388" />

However, the most significant opportunity may be the company's approximately 25,806 one-time customers.
These customers have already completed a purchase, meaning the company has an existing relationship with them. Their average revenue per customer in 2026 is approximately $86, compared with approximately $132 for repeat customers and $184  for frequent customers, and like its companion segments, it has been steadily rising with time. This creates a clear question for the Financial team to ask: 

“Can TheLook increase the number of customers who progress from one-time to repeat purchasing?”

**Recommendation**

Increase CLV through customer retention and repeat-purchase initiatives over relying exclusively on new customer acquisition.

For this focus, the Marketing Team should look at methods to re-engage one time customers, such as post-purchase email campaigns, personalized product recommendations, and re-engagement campaigns.

As these develop and 2026 concludes, the Finance team should remain monitoring revenue per customer, and customer profitability, alongside repeat purchase rate changes before and after retention campaign launches. Looking at incremental profit changes from these campaigns will also determine whether the investments are profitable and capable of sustained growth. 

### Priority 2: Core Markets & Testing South Korea
**Findings**
<img width="2756" height="2176" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7f70eb9-4ff0-4572-bbc3-30c6c736cbc3" />

China, the United States, and Brazil are the company's strongest markets by customer count, revenue, and gross profit. In 2026, these 3 combined accounted for 37% of TheLook’s 2026 sales revenue and historically they also demonstrated substantial growth. TheLook already has customers, purchasing activity and revenue in these locations, and they have potential to remain theLooks’ main revenue drivers in 2027 if the October 2026 spike remains steady.

**Recommendation**

China, Brazil and the U.S. should be considered TheLook’s core geographic markets, rather than being treated as opportunities requiring validation. Therefore, it is recommended to continue investment into these markets to efficiently expand, but where possible look into incremental investment to test emerging markets in South Korea. Key in on customer retention, high performing campaigns and maintaining market share. As for expansion, test additional paid media, and localized email campaigns as those performed well in South Korea. 

### Priority 3: High-Performing Products
**Findings**

Revenue is concentrated among a handful of product categories, the five leading being:
1. Outerwear and Coats
2. Jeans
3. Sweaters
4. Swim
5. Suits & Sports Coats

<img width="3006" height="398" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/911d1e8c-0275-4915-b88a-84bf9f8ab6fd" />


Even if we dig deeper into the number of orders made by users in 2026, the top 5 categories are within the top 10 ranks of order counts.These categories are already performing well, and prioritizing high investment into items customers are already buying in high concentration may be truncating possible product portfolio optimizations. 

**Recommendation**

Use product-level performance to optimize the portfolio rather than simply increasing investment in top-selling categories.

Outerwear & Coats, Jeans, Sweaters, Swim & Suits, and Sports Coats generate over 30% of product revenue. But that doesn't necessarily mean they deserve more investment. They may already be receiving appropriate investment and simply have stronger underlying demand. 

Finance should work with the Product and Sales Teams to evaluate profitability, growth, customer demand, inventory efficiency, and returns. Products should then be categorized as Expand, Protect, Test, or Rationalize based on their expected contribution to future growth.

### Priority 4: Increasing Organic Traffic 

**Findings**

<img width="3006" height="318" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a1f84f6-89ec-4acf-91e6-29f989681e77" />
The traffic channels that TheLook has are performing well in terms of revenue and user volume, however there is room for improvement. In 2026, Email and AdWords generated the largest share of revenue and orders, making them the company's leading traffic sources based on the available data. Facebook and YouTube contribute additional revenue and order volume, indicating that they may offer opportunities for continued channel development while Organic Traffic currently contributes less revenue and fewer orders than the leading channels, suggesting an opportunity to improve organic customer acquisition in 2027. 

It is not uncommon for organic traffic to be low, especially in ecommerce retail industry, but the rise in AI overviews means the SEO realm is evolving and TheLook may be missing critical methods to ensure the website is being picked up by the algorithm.

Current findings are based primarily on revenue, demographic, and order-volume patterns. Additional engagement and marketing performance metrics are needed to determine the efficiency and profitability of each channel for specific marketing channel investment strategies. 

**Recommendations
**

Optimize established marketing channels while strengthening Organic Traffic.

Maintain support for Email and AdWords given their existing revenue and order contributions, while continuing to evaluate opportunities to improve Facebook and YouTube performance. Increase strategic focus on Organic Traffic through SEO improvements, content development, and product-page optimization to build a stronger long-term acquisition channel.

Before making significant 2027 budget reallocations, Finance and Marketing should incorporate additional metrics, including click-through rate (CTR), conversion rate, customer acquisition cost (CAC), return on ad spend (ROAS), and gross profit by channel. These measures will help determine which channels generate the strongest financial returns and where incremental marketing investment is most justified.


The analysis of TheLook's 2019–2026 ecommerce performance highlights several opportunities to strengthen the company's existing revenue base while positioning the business for continued growth in 2027.

### Conclusion & Next Steps

Customer retention represents a significant opportunity to increase customer lifetime value by converting more one-time purchasers into repeat customers. Geographically, China, the United States, and Brazil remain the company's established revenue markets, while South Korea presents an opportunity for controlled expansion. Product investment should focus on optimizing the existing portfolio based on profitability, demand, and operational efficiency rather than simply increasing investment in the highest-revenue categories. Finally, established marketing channels such as Email and AdWords should remain important components of the company's strategy, alongside efforts to strengthen organic traffic through SEO and content development.

While the findings provide a foundation for identifying investment priorities, additional analysis is necessary before making significant budget reallocations. In particular, incorporating customer acquisition costs, engagement metrics, channel-level profitability, inventory turnover, and product return rates would provide a more complete picture of where incremental investment can generate the greatest financial return.

Moving forward, Finance should work alongside Marketing, Product, Sales, and Supply Chain to establish measurable performance targets, evaluate proposed initiatives, and monitor their financial impact throughout 2027. Progress should be assessed through incremental gross profit, customer lifetime value, repeat purchase rates, market growth, product profitability, and marketing efficiency.

Ultimately, TheLook's 2027 investment strategy should not be driven solely by where the company has generated the most revenue historically, but by where it can generate the greatest sustainable and incremental value moving forward.
